## Project Overview

ShopShot is an AI-powered product photography platform that generates professional e-commerce images from user-uploaded product photos. Users upload a single product image and receive 11 variations including lifestyle shots, white backgrounds, in-use scenarios, and more.

### Core Features
- **AI Image Generation** - 11 variation types per upload using Gemini/Vertex AI
- **360° Video Generation** - Veo 3 Fast spinning product videos
- **Dual Credit System** - Standard (cheaper/faster) and Pro (better quality) credits
- **User Authentication** - Email/password + Google OAuth
- **Stripe Payments** - Subscriptions + credit pack purchases
- **History/Sessions** - All generations saved and viewable
- **Admin Dashboard** - User management, credit adjustments, analytics

## Database Schema (D1)

### Key Tables

**users**
- `id`, `email`, `password_hash`, `name`, `phone`
- `cheaper_credits`, `better_credits` (dual credit system)
- `subscription_status`, `subscription_plan`
- `stripe_customer_id`, `google_id`
- `email_verified`, `role` (user/admin)
- `marketing_consent`

**sessions** (generation sessions)
- `id`, `user_id`, `original_image` (base64)
- `source_type`, `source_url`
- `video_url`, `video_type` (for 360 videos)
- `model`, `credits_charged`

**generated_images**
- `id`, `session_id`, `variation_type`, `variation_index`
- `image_data` (compressed base64)
- `model`

**generated_videos**
- `id`, `user_id`, `session_id`
- `video_url`, `status`, `error_message`

**credit_transactions**
- `id`, `user_id`, `type`, `amount`
- `credit_type` (cheaper/better)
- `description`, `reference_id`

**stripe_events** (webhook deduplication)

## Credit System

### Two Credit Types
1. **Cheaper (Standard)** - Uses Flash/Nano Banana model (~25s generation)
2. **Better (Pro)** - Uses Pro/Nano Banana Pro model (~60-120s generation)

### Credit Costs
- **Per Image**: 1 credit (of selected type)
- **360° Video**: 40 cheaper credits

### Signup Bonus
- 10 cheaper credits + 5 better credits (free)

### Subscription Plans
| Plan | Monthly | Cheaper Credits | Better Credits |
|------|---------|-----------------|----------------|
| Free | £0 | 10 (signup) | 5 (signup) |
| Standard | £49 | 500/mo | 45/mo |
| Pro | £99 | 300/mo | 175/mo |

### Credit Packs (One-time purchase)
| Price | Cheaper | Better |
|-------|---------|--------|
| £25 | 400 | 100 |
| £50 | 800 | 200 |
| £75 | 1200 | 300 |
| £100 | 1600 | 400 |
